# Release 1 Demo Script

1. Login as `admin@invox.local`.
2. Show the dashboard and Release 1 scope.
3. Open Counterparties and show CIPA Holdings Group, Demo Supplier Ltd, and Syndicate SCF Fund.
4. Show KYC/KYB fields, documents, and audit evidence.
5. Open Programmes and show CIPA Supplier Finance Programme.
6. Show supplier and funder participants plus configured limits.
7. Open Invoices and show `INV-DEMO-001`.
8. Approve an invoice or show the approved invoice state.
9. Generate an early-payment offer.
10. Accept the offer as supplier workflow.
11. Allocate funding.
12. Mark disbursed and show outbound payment.
13. Mark collected and show inbound payment.
14. Show ledger entries and confirm balanced debit/credit totals.
15. Show reconciliation records.
16. Open Reports/Operations dashboard and show exposure release.
17. Open Audit/Approvals and show maker-checker/audit trail.

Keep the demo focused on approved payables finance. Do not demo deferred products.
